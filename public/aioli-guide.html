Condiment Reference

Aioli Guide

Base: mayo (Kirkland or Chosen Foods avocado oil). Mix, taste, adjust, refrigerate 30+ min before serving. Each batch ≈ 1/2 cup — scale up freely.

Colors are Pantone-inspired flavor pairings, not certified Pantone matches
Savory / Bold
01Garlic Parmesan
Bone White · #F4EDE0
  • 1/2 cup mayo
  • 1 tsp roasted garlic (or 1 clove fresh, minced)
  • 2 tbsp grated parmesan
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
Great with chicken chunks or as a dip for potato cubes.
02Sriracha Lime
Fiery Red #DA3E30 + Lime Punch #A8C23A
  • 1/2 cup mayo
  • 1–2 tbsp sriracha (to taste)
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • 1 tsp honey
You've already got sriracha and honey on hand — just need the mayo.
03Chipotle Honey
Chipotle Amber · #B5651D
  • 1/2 cup mayo
  • 1 tbsp chipotle in adobo, minced
  • 1 tbsp honey
  • 1 tsp lime juice
Smoky-sweet, great on tacos or drizzled over chicken.
04Smoky Paprika
Chili Pepper · #9B3922
  • 1/2 cup mayo
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1/2 tsp garlic powder
  • 1 tsp vinegar (white or apple cider)
Closest DIY dupe to the Tari Rocoto flavor — good stopgap without the bottle.
05Curry
Golden Spice · #E4A227
  • 1/2 cup mayo
  • 1 tsp curry powder
  • 1 tsp honey
Unexpectedly good with chicken and potatoes.
06Buffalo
Fiesta · #DD4124
  • 1/2 cup mayo
  • 2 tbsp Frank's RedHot
  • 1 tsp melted butter
Dip for chicken chunks, or spread on a sandwich. Butter's already stocked.
07Basil Pesto
Basil · #4A5D23
  • 1/2 cup mayo
  • 2 tbsp pesto (jarred or fresh-blended)
Good on sandwiches, or as a dip for pretzel nuggets if you're feeling experimental.
08Everything Bagel
Toasted Sesame · #B08D57
  • 1/2 cup mayo
  • 1 tbsp everything bagel seasoning
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
No cooking required — great on eggs or a quick sandwich spread.
09BBQ Ranch
Chestnut · #8B4225
  • 1/2 cup mayo
  • 2 tbsp BBQ sauce
  • 1/2 tsp ranch seasoning (dry packet or homemade blend)
Works as a dip or burger spread.
Herbal / Bright
10Classic Garlic Herb
Sage · #9CAF88
  • 1/2 cup mayo
  • 1 clove garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp fresh parsley, chopped
  • 1 tsp chives, chopped
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
All-purpose green aioli — good on almost anything.
11Basil Lemon
Fresh Basil · #6B8E23
  • 1/2 cup mayo
  • 2 tbsp fresh basil, finely chopped or blended
  • 1 tsp lemon zest
  • 1/2 tsp garlic, minced
Bright, works on sandwiches or grilled chicken.
12Dill & Lemon
Dill · #A9BA6B
  • 1/2 cup mayo
  • 1 tbsp fresh or 1 tsp dried dill
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1/2 tsp garlic, minced
Classic with fish, also great with tuna.
13Chive & Black Pepper
Chive Green #5B7B4E + Pepper Fleck #2B2B2B
  • 1/2 cup mayo
  • 1 tbsp fresh chives, chopped
  • 1/2 tsp cracked black pepper
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
Simple, clean flavor — good all-purpose option.
14Cilantro Lime
Green Flash · #7CB518
  • 1/2 cup mayo
  • 2 tbsp cilantro, chopped
  • 1 tbsp lime juice
  • 1/2 tsp garlic, minced
  • Pinch of cumin
Great with the fajita bowl or tuna poke bowl already in the main recipe file.
15Rosemary Garlic
Rosemary · #2E4636
  • 1/2 cup mayo
  • 1 tsp fresh rosemary, finely minced
  • 1 tsp roasted garlic
Earthier profile, good with potatoes.
16Tarragon
Tarragon · #87A96B
  • 1/2 cup mayo
  • 1 tsp fresh tarragon, chopped
  • 1 tsp white wine vinegar
  • 1/2 tsp dijon mustard (if on hand)
French-leaning, great on chicken sandwiches.
17Mint & Lemon
Mint · #98D8C1
  • 1/2 cup mayo
  • 1 tbsp fresh mint, chopped
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1/2 tsp garlic, minced
Unexpected but refreshing on grilled meats.
18Herbes de Provence
Provence Herb #8E9775 + Lavender #A89CC8
  • 1/2 cup mayo
  • 1 tsp herbes de Provence
  • 1 tsp lemon zest
Earthy, floral — works on almost anything roasted.
19Sage & Garlic
Desert Sage · #B2AC88
  • 1/2 cup mayo
  • 1 tsp fresh sage, finely chopped
  • 1/2 tsp garlic, minced
  • Pinch of black pepper
Richer profile, good with potatoes or poultry.
New Frontier — Pantry-Inspired
20Harissa Aioli
Harissa Red · #A83232
  • 1/2 cup mayo
  • 1-2 tbsp harissa (to taste)
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
  • 1/2 tsp garlic, minced (optional — harissa is already garlicky)
Confirmed go-to burger sauce. Scale the harissa up for more heat.
21Chili Crisp Aioli
Chili Crisp Amber · #8B3A1A
  • 1/2 cup mayo
  • 1-2 tbsp Lao Gan Ma chili crisp (spoon in the crispy bits, not just the oil)
  • 1 tsp rice vinegar
Crunchy, savory, a little numbing. Great on eggs, burgers, or as a dip for chicken chunks.
22Ginger Scallion Aioli
Scallion Green · #6B8F3C
  • 1/2 cup mayo
  • 1 tsp fresh ginger, finely grated
  • 2 tbsp scallions, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp rice vinegar
  • Pinch of salt
Bright and savory — excellent on chicken, or as a dip alongside the chicken chunks.
23Ancho Aioli
Ancho Brick · #8C3B2E
  • 1/2 cup mayo
  • 1 tsp ancho chile powder
  • 1 tsp lime juice
  • Pinch of salt
Mild, earthy heat with a raisin-like sweetness. Good on tacos or burgers.
24Preserved Lemon Aioli
Preserved Lemon · #C9A227
  • 1/2 cup mayo
  • 1 tsp preserved lemon paste
  • 1/2 tsp garlic, minced
Salty, tangy, a little funky in the best way. Great on sandwiches or with fish.
☆Shortcut: Tari Cheat Aioli
Aji Amarillo #E3A857 + Aji Rocoto #B5502E
  • No recipe needed — stir either Tari sauce directly into mayo, 1:1 to taste.
Aji Amarillo: bright, fruity, mild. Aji Rocoto: smoky, bolder. Instant aioli from bottles already in rotation.
