# Owner context

Matters when adding recipes or writing copy. Carried over from the original
project handoff so it isn't only in chat history.

## Equipment

- **Ninja Foodi XL flip-down air fryer** — top burner element, no basket.
  Always sheet pan, never the crisper tray. Firm preference, don't suggest
  otherwise.
- **Galanz manual-dial microwave** — Low / Defrost / M.Low / Med / M.High /
  High. M.Low ≈ PL4. Give dial positions, not just power levels.
- **Anyday Deep Dish** for all microwave rice.
- Full kitchen upstairs but inconvenient; most cooking is a basement setup.
  Favor low-mess, low-cleanup methods.

## Calibrated baselines — don't re-derive these

- Chicken breast: 370°F. Two fully thawed = 22–25 min, probe at 20. Three
  partially thawed ≈ 29 min.
- Frozen broccoli: 400°F, 13 min. Wants more char; 410°F is the next test.
- Egg bites in silicone molds: 300°F, 10–11 min. Do not exceed 300°F —
  gray-green interior is iron sulfide from overheating (safe, but avoidable).
- Anyday brown rice: 1 cup rice : 1⅓ cups water — Anyday official, not the
  stovetop 1:2. Rinse first. M.Low, 30 min.

## Preferences

- Savory and umami-forward; bright/citrus over warm spices in condiments and
  jams.
- Cumin is fine — no need to flag it.
- Kewpie sesame dressing goes on salad greens only. Not rice bowls, not
  quesadillas, not anything else.
- Herbes de Provence: lavender can read soapy — start at ¼ tsp, bloom in oil.
- Budget-conscious, does cost-per-dollar analysis. Shops Costco (Executive),
  Wegmans, and Aldi via Instacart.

## Working style

- Casual, direct, dry humor. Concise answers, no over-explanation.
- Pushes back clearly when something's wrong. Integrate the correction and
  move on — no over-apologizing.
- **Standing rule: if you are not close to certain of a factual claim
  (ratios, specs, temperatures, product details), verify before answering.**
  No guesstimates presented as fact. This rule exists because it was
  violated repeatedly.
